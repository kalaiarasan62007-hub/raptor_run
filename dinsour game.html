Hand Control Guide

Confidence --
SHOW YOUR HAND

Head control: turn or lean your head LEFT / RIGHT = change lane · nod UP = jump · nod DOWN = duck · open your mouth = energy shield. Hand, head and keyboard all work together.

This game needs WebGL. Open it in a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Hold jump for a tall jump. Hop over low obstacles, swap lanes around tall ones, duck under pterodactyls. Red circles on the ground mark where meteors will land. Gems score points, bubbles give a shield, hearts give a life. On a touch screen you can also swipe. Turn on Camera, then move your real hand. Quick sideways movement changes lane by one step, quick upward movement jumps and quick downward movement ducks. Movement is detected from motion, not from hand position, so bringing your hand back does not move the dinosaur back. Closed fist, peace sign, three fingers and thumbs up trigger special powers. Fist, peace sign, three fingers and thumbs up show as special signs (Q, E, 3, T on the keyboard). Press C to change the camera view. Head control (button Head or key N): turn or lean your head left or right to change lane, nod up to jump, nod down to duck, open your mouth for a shield. Hand, head and keyboard can all be used at the same time.
